Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about shopping with Luma Deals.

How do I place an order?

Browse the shop, open the product you want, add it to your cart and proceed to checkout. Enter your contact details and complete delivery information carefully. Review your order before submitting it.

Which payment methods are available?

Payment options shown at checkout are the options currently available for your order. If Cash on Delivery (COD) is displayed, you can select it and pay when the parcel is delivered, according to the order instructions.

Where does Luma Deals deliver?

Delivery availability and any applicable charges are shown during checkout or confirmed by our team. Please provide a complete address, including house or building, street, area and city, to help the courier deliver your parcel.

How can I track my order?

Sign in and check your Orders section for available order updates. If tracking details are not shown yet, contact customer support with your order number.

Can I cancel or change an order?

Contact customer support as soon as possible and share your order number. A change or cancellation may not be possible after the order has been processed or handed to the courier.

What if my item arrives damaged or incorrect?

Please contact customer support promptly with your order number and clear photos of the item and packaging. Our team will review the issue and explain the available next steps.
